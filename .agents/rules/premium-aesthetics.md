# Premium Aesthetic Guidelines

When generating UI, writing CSS, or proposing designs for this project, you MUST strictly adhere to the following luxury aesthetic rules:

1. **Brand Vibe**: Minimalist, high-end, luxury (think Apple meets Tesla or high-end editorial).
2. **Typography**: 
   - `Playfair Display` (Serif) for expressive, large headers, numbers, and hero text.
   - `Inter` (Sans-serif) for technical, sharp, wide-tracked uppercase sub-headers and body text.
3. **Geometry**: STRICTLY sharp corners (`border-radius: 0;`). No rounded corners unless explicitly requested.
4. **Animations & Interactions**: Use slow, deliberate, and smooth transitions (e.g., `0.4s ease-in-out`). Avoid bouncy or cartoonish animations. Favor sophisticated micro-interactions like magnetic hovers or slow background gradients.
5. **Textures & Depth**: Use glassmorphism (`backdrop-filter`) elegantly, subtle metallic/gold borders (`rgba(194, 165, 126, 0.4)`), and cinematic dip-to-black or slow-fade transitions.
